Tiled Map Builder

Describe a map. Get a Tiled file.

An open AI model reads your description and builds an isometric map with this project's MCP tools: rooms, corridors, doors, traps, chests and spawns. Preview it here, then download a .zip that opens in the Tiled editor.

Everything runs on your Hugging Face account: the AI model calls are billed to you (usually a few cents per map, often covered by your monthly inference credits). Your token stays in this browser tab and is only used for your own requests.
